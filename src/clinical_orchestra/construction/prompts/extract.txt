Independently extract the indexed report into two linked chronologies:
(1) the source-supported physical clinical course, and
(2) successive information phases available to the treating team.
Do not generate benchmark questions or consult another extractor's answers.

Record every patient-specific fact with exact supporting quotes and source span IDs.
The claim must be self-contained and preserve relevant dates, durations, values and units that
were known at that fact's information phase. A separate clinical_time may locate an event in
retrospect; do not put retrospective timing into an earlier visible claim. Record later-discovered
timing as a new fact at its disclosure phase instead.
Use kind=context for initial/history/examination information, and action, result, diagnosis,
progression or response for later typed events. The indexed patient is patient_id=patient;
family history is context about that patient, not another patient's clinical trajectory.
If multiple patient trajectories cannot be separated, report unresolved and do not merge them.

Create knowledge phases with contiguous ranks starting at zero. Within a phase the source does
not establish a finer order. The phases must have evidence for chronology; paragraph order alone
is insufficient. Each fact needs its own known_evidence. If availability is unknown, known_phase
is null; never assume that all opening-paragraph facts were known on admission.

clinical_time describes when the event physically occurred or the specimen time a finding concerns.
If not established, use description=unknown and evidence=[]. clinical_after contains only explicitly
supported earlier fact IDs and need not produce a total order. A history learned on day three
about pre-admission symptoms is an earlier clinical event but day-three information.
Availability to the team is a proxy unless actual acknowledgment is documented. Preserve suspected
beliefs as considered, not confirmed patient facts. Append corrections; never rewrite earlier states.

Distinguish observed, ordered, performed, pending, considered, confirmed, corrected and canceled.
Link every result to its action and every response to its treatment using linked_action_id.
For response/progression facts, record an outcome-neutral endpoint such as day 7 or the three-month
follow-up, with evidence, if stated. Otherwise use null. Improvement after treatment is not proof
of causation. Do not infer an unreported onset date from the final diagnosis.

Coverage must include every supplied span exactly once: represented with fact_ids, not_patient_information
with a reason, or unresolved. Do not omit patient information just because it seems unimportant.
Thesis may contain up to three author claims with evidence and supporting fact IDs; this is hidden
metadata, not permission to select or invent early clues. List unresolved chronology or source conflicts.
