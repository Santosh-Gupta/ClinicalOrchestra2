Audit the primary evidence graph and candidate items against the entire indexed source.
A second independent extraction is supplied as a witness, not as a vote or authoritative answer.
First derive each item's source-reported target independently. There is no proposed gold option.

Review every primary fact, knowledge phase and source span exactly once. Check claim entailment,
patient identity, status, negation, quantities/units, specimen links and event/knowledge timing.
Check the independent extraction for facts or timing omitted by the primary extraction. In coverage_reviews,
supported means coverage is adequate, including justified nonpatient exclusions; unresolved omissions
of patient information must be unknown or contradicted. Confirming that quotations merely exist is
not sufficient. Flag contradictory tables/prose or unresolved chronology. Do not repair from medical knowledge.

For each item, check EVERY visible fact's availability at the cutoff and list its ID in
visible_fact_ids_checked. Check for hidden results in action wording, retrospective interpretations,
answer-bearing endpoint text, and omitted pre-cut information. Distinguish a clinical development
from the later discovery of an earlier event. Ensure the question's prediction_axis matches this.
If target chronology is not uniquely supported at the requested granularity, reject the item.

Derive the target from its source evidence, then map it to exactly one option ID, including OTHER
when none matches. Do not insert the gold or change options. Reject synonym overlaps and cases where
multiple options fit the recorded target. A candidate can be a reasonable clinical choice yet be
incorrect as the specified documented completion. Do not infer negatives from source silence.

Use supported only for adequately supported facts/phases/items. Use unknown or contradicted otherwise,
with reasons and exact source evidence where available. For an item with no defensible label, use
answer_id=null. Put case-wide fatal defects in hard_defects and unresolved source disputes in unresolved.
Agreement between models is not evidence. Return only the specified complete audit schema.
